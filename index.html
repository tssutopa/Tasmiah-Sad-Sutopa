A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Connecting statistics with meaningful research.

My academic work brings together statistical training, university teaching, and applied research on health and social questions.

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Statistics at the University of Dhaka, Bangladesh, and a PhD candidate in the School of Public Health and Social Work at Queensland University of Technology (QUT).

I completed both my BSc and MSc in Statistics at the University of Dhaka, ranking first in each programme. My experience includes teaching undergraduate and postgraduate students, supervising an undergraduate coursework project, collaborating on research, and presenting work at academic conferences.

My doctoral supervisory team includes Associate Professor Susanna Cramb, Associate Professor Darren Wraith, and Professor Cate Cameron.
